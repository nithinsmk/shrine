SXSE × Chinabot × _Subkontinent welcome you to a night of mediation between infra and ultra, nether and heavens, truths and majicks.

Allow the pilandok, the mouse-deer, shapeshifter and crosser of boundaries, to escort you through voids and shines. Follow close, but never too close, and trust the path it takes.

Come sway with us on a night that yearns for transformation, transition and trickery.

Leave your reds at home, lest you are lost before you reach the temporary shrine of our collective making.
